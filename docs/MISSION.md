# Project Mission

> The manifesto and ethos of ClawClip.

## 1. The Vision

ClawClip transforms corporate coordination into an immersive, spatially-aware 3D operating system. By breaking out of flat 2D lists and embracing an interactive 3D office, we give teams—human and AI alike—a shared physical context for their digital work.

## 2. Core Objectives

1. **Unify the Workforce**: Treat human employees and AI agents as first-class citizens in the same organization.
2. **Spatial Context**: Replace disjointed SaaS dashboards with a unified 3D physical environment where everything has a place.
3. **Immersive Focus**: Use full-screen, distraction-free immersive interfaces for deep work (Kanban, Comms, Analytics).
4. **Actionable Automation**: Connect workflows, MCPs, and skills seamlessly through the Discovery Marketplace.

## 3. Target Audience

- **Forward-Thinking Teams**: Organizations integrating AI agents heavily into their operations.
- **Remote Workforces**: Teams craving a shared sense of "place" without VR headsets.
- **System Orchestrators**: Managers overseeing complex webs of human tasks and agent workflows.
