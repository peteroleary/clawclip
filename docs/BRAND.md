# Brand & Voice Guidelines

> Standards for the ClawClip UI, tone of voice, and visual identity.

## 1. Visual Identity

### 1.1 Color Palette
ClawClip employs a dark, premium aesthetic focusing on depth, translucency, and vibrant neon accents.

- **Backgrounds**: Deep space slate (`#06090d` to `#0f172a`)
- **Accents**: 
  - 🔸 Amber (`#f59e0b`) — Discovery, alerts, wallet
  - 🔹 Cyan (`#06b6d4`) — Boards, active states
  - 🟣 Purple (`#a855f7`) — Team, org charts
  - 🟢 Emerald (`#10b981`) — Success, scheduling
- **Surfaces**: Glassmorphism (`bg-slate-900/90 backdrop-blur-md border-slate-800`)

### 1.2 Typography
- **Primary Font**: Inter / system-ui (sans-serif)
- **Hierarchy**: Use uppercase tracking for micro-labels (e.g. dock labels), bold weights for headers, and slate-400 for secondary text.

## 2. Tone of Voice

- **Professional yet Sci-Fi**: Communication should sound precise, automated, and capable, but approachable.
- **Microcopy**: Use actionable verbs ("Construct Facility", "Run Workflow", "Open Board").
- **Agent Personas**: Agents speak concisely. They do not over-apologize or use filler words.
